Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains what data eatwithsamk.com collects and why. It's short on purpose.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages get read and how visitors find the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymized usage data (pages visited, general location, device type) — it does not collect names, emails, or other information that identifies you personally.

Advertising

This site displays ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies generally at aboutads.info.

Newsletter

If you sign up for the newsletter, your email address is used only to send you updates from Eat With Sam K — it's never sold or shared with anyone else. You can unsubscribe from any email at any time.

Cookies

Cookies on this site are used only for the analytics and advertising purposes described above. You can block or delete cookies through your browser's settings; some site features may not work as well if you do.

Third-Party Content

Some pages embed content from Instagram, which may set its own cookies and collect data according to Instagram's own privacy policy — that's between you and Meta, not something this site controls.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via any of the social links on this site.

This is a general-purpose policy for a small personal site, not a substitute for legal advice — if you have specific compliance requirements, have a lawyer review it.
