Hi, I'm Sam 👋

The face (and stomach) behind Eat With Sam K

I've always loved trying new restaurants, whether it's a hidden local gem or a place everyone has been talking about. Living in Nashville and traveling across the world, I realized there are so many incredible spots to eat, but it can be hard to know which ones are actually worth your time and money. That's why I started Eat With Sam K.

My goal is simple: to help people discover great food through honest, unbiased reviews. Every meal you see on this site is paid for with my own money. I don't accept paid reviews or change my opinions in exchange for free food. If I recommend a restaurant, it's because I genuinely enjoyed it.

I created this site to become more than just another food blog. My vision is to build the most trusted restaurant guide — a place where locals and visitors can quickly find the best burgers, pizza, date-night spots, hidden gems, and everything in between. As I continue exploring Nashville, this site will keep growing with new reviews, rankings, neighborhood guides, and curated lists.

Thanks for following along and supporting Eat With Sam K. I hope my reviews help you find your next favorite meal.

How I Rate

Every review is scored consistently — the same criteria whether it's a $4 slice or a $200 tasting menu: taste, value, atmosphere, and service. You'll find the full breakdown on each individual review page. A 9+ means book a flight; anything below 7 gets explained, not just scored.

Where to Find More

Video reviews go up on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts first — the map and Best Of guides here are where everything lives long-term, searchable and organized by city.
